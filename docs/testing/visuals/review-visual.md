# Visual review of the presentation pass

Independent review of `feat/visuals` (all builders merged, head `e853686`). Checked live against a fresh stack (API 8722, vite 5722) with four trials: psilocybin seed 42, 500 applicants, walked to D62 with manual approvals (Dosing), auto-approved to D90 (Dosing D90, Follow-up), and two predose trials (baseline, five fixes plus shortlist). Studio used `tests/e2e/visuals/studio_report_NCT03866174.json`. Each chart was measured at 1440 light, 1440 dark and 390 (2x, touch) with a script that reads computed colours and resolves `var(--...)`, blends opacity onto the actual chart surface, and computes WCAG ratios. It also checks SVG text clipping, label overlap, element overflow, `scrollWidth`, em dashes and US spellings. Interaction checks covered tap to open and tap outside on every tooltip type, "View data" at 390, a 120-step Tab walk per page, and `prefers-reduced-motion`. For colour blindness, a deuteranopia and a protanopia `feColorMatrix` filter was applied to `<html>`.

Evidence lives in `docs/testing/visuals/review/` (`cb-deut-*`, `cb-prot-*` colour-blind shots at 1440 light, `<page>-<chart>-390.png`, `focus-*`, `tip-*`, `viewdata-*`). The side-by-side shots are in `docs/testing/visuals/compare/` (see its README).

Chart surface in light mode is `rgb(245,243,241)`; in dark mode `rgb(23,22,20)`.

## Passed

- **Takeaway titles.** Every chart's title is a sentence computed from data, for example "Site B raters flagged for drift on day 47; remote raters approved day 48", "Reported −6.41 MADRS points; after cross-trial calibration −2.95, interval crosses 0" and "49 of 500 applicants enrolled: 49 of 49 eligible, 0 of 56 flagged". No "(SYNTHETIC)" is left in titles; there is one quiet "Synthetic" note per chart.
- **Colour never alone.** The alerted site and adjusted estimates use orange with circles plus a dot and words in the panel title. Violet uses squares, and context sites are dashed or dotted with end labels. Under the deuteranopia and protanopia filters (`review/cb-deut-*`, `cb-prot-*`), orange becomes olive and violet stays blue, and every distinction still reads through shape, line style or label.
- **Dark mode.** Text contrast passes on every chart (smoke 6.89:1 or better on `#171614`). Lines and markers use the dark violet and orange tokens. No hex leaks were found in the colours read.
- **Horizontal scroll.** No horizontal page scroll at 390 on Studio, Dosing, the Dosing drawer, Follow-up, the Follow-up drawer or Screening (`scrollWidth == 390`). Design review is the exception (V-1).
- **Other 390 layout.** Apart from V-6, V-8 (3 px) and V-9, no clipped SVG text at 390.
- **Tooltips on Dosing, CUSUM and effect-estimates.** They are in words ("Day 26, Site A: 8 of 17 decisive guesses correct in the last 3 batches (47%), 95% interval 26% to 69% ..."; "Cross-trial calibration: −2.95 MADRS points, 95% interval −7.22 to 1.33, crosses 0."). They stay inside 390 (x 67 to 288, 46 to 326), open on tap and close on a tap outside.
- **View data.** Present on every chart. At 390 the drawer table on cusum-site has a caption equal to the title, `th scope="col"` and 29 rows. It marks derived columns "(derived)" and adds a note that explains the derivation. The table scrolls inside its own focusable region with no page scroll, and Escape closes it.
- **aria-labels.** Present and meaningful on every chart: they give the latest values, intervals, events and reference lines, not just the chart name.
- **Reduced motion.** No animation runs under `reduce`. Recharts animations are off, and the mount fade is behind `no-preference` only.
- **Writing.** No em dashes and no US spellings were found in any visible chart text across 14 charts and 3 settings. Unicode minus is used for negatives.
- **Consistency.** All charts share the flat surface, Open Sans, the title, subtitle and "Synthetic" header, and the quiet "View data" button. Violet and orange appear only inside charts. Glass appears only on the nav.
- **Earlier problems now fixed.** Before vs after, the rater-accuracy chart moved from one overlaid plot with a legend to labelled small multiples with Wilson bands and hollow small-n markers. The effect plots gained "No effect" and direction words. Curves became step-after, the "Expected with working blinding 0.6" line is relabelled "Alarm reference 60%", and the screening funnel became a CONSORT-style flow.
