### What this means (read before quoting any number)

* **Naive is biased when blinding fails.** In the planted world the reported effect overstates the benefit by 2.3 points on average (truth 4), and its 95% interval contains the truth only 45% of the time (200 seeds).
* **The within-trial estimate recovers the truth when its assumption holds.** Bias -0.35, coverage 94%. With no bias planted it stays close (bias +0.43), so it does little harm to a clean trial.
* **It fails under reverse causation, in the direction we warned about.** When guesses follow improvement it removes real benefit: bias +2.00 points (about half of the true 4), coverage 85%. This is why the report calls the adjusted value a conservative bound.
* **The cross-trial calibration over-corrects on these trials.** Bias +1.42 in the planted world and +3.82 with no bias at all. It treats all unblinding as bias, and here people guess right from side effects even when their belief does not change their score. The calibration is also SYNTHETIC. We show it for transparency and do not use it as the headline adjusted value when the within-trial estimate exists.
* **Small trials cannot use the within-trial method.** With 24 participants it was estimable in 5 of 200 simulated trials, because the vault suppresses cells under 5. The report then says so instead of guessing.
* The headline table uses 20 seeds as planned; the 200-seed run below agrees in direction and size.
